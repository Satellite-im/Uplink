✦ Uplink

Sign in once.
Synced to every device.

Messenger and Instagram have no API — Uplink uses your browser's own sign-in. This extension captures it and syncs it, sealed, to all your devices.

  • Log in to Messenger or Instagram in your browser — your phone and laptop log in too
  • Sealed end to end — the relay stores only a blob it can't read
  • No cURL, no copy-paste, no tracking — open source
