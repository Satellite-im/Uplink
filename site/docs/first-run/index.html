Getting started

The first time you open it

A username and a password, your recovery words, and on Android your texts.

The first time you open Uplink, it asks for a username and a password, and that's it.

The steps

  1. Start with your texts (Android only): whether Uplink takes over your texts (below), or Skip this step to use Uplink without them. A voice reads the page; switch it off with Voice off at the top.
  2. Pick a username and a password: the username is how people find you; the password makes your Uplink account, so you can sign in on another device. Already have one? The same screen has Already have an account? under it: tap Sign in.
  3. Your recovery words: write down the 12 words, then tap them in order to check, or tap Skip this check.

Your name, picture, look and bubble colour are in Settings whenever you want them: Your profile and number.

Texting with Uplink, or beside Google Messages

On an Android phone, the texts page asks how Uplink should handle your texts. Either way, nothing is deleted: your texts stay in Android's own store.

ChoiceWhat happensGood for
Set as default SMS appUplink sends and receives your texts and picture messages. Android asks you to confirm.Making Uplink your one texting app.
Use beside Google MessagesGoogle Messages stays your texting app; Uplink reads your texts and its notifications and shows them alongside everything else.Trying Uplink first, or keeping RCS chats. See Beside Google Messages.

Change your mind any time: Settings › Messages, or Android's Settings › Apps › Default apps.

Coming from Google Messages or Samsung Messages

If you make Uplink your texting app, a card says Coming from Google Messages? Do this first. Do it: while RCS chats are on in your old app, people who use RCS keep sending you chats that never arrive as texts, and look like they've gone missing.

In Google Messages, that's your profile picture, then Messages settings › RCS chats, off.

What it asks for, and why

AskedWhy
Texts (SMS and picture messages)To show, send and receive them.
ContactsFor names and pictures, and to find which of your contacts use Uplink. Read on the phone only.
NotificationsTo tell you when a message comes.
Microphone and cameraOnly when you record a voice message, take a picture, or join a call.
