Marketplace guidelines

What gets a plugin listed, what keeps one out, and how reports and withdrawals work.

Plugins in the Marketplace read people's texts, so they're held to more than working. These are what review looks for. Sending a plugin means saying, in the form, "I made this plugin, and it follows the Marketplace guidelines".

What gets a plugin listed

  • It does what it says. Its name, short line and description say what it does, and it does that and nothing else.
  • Its phrases are precise. They match the texts it's for and not others. A phrase that's one short word, or a word like "your", catches far too much: Try the samples shows what yours catches, and a dinner plan or a sign-in code should come out untouched.
  • What marks texts read or silences them is behind a setting, off until the person switches it on. The file format makes sure of that, so it's only a matter of saying clearly, in the setting's words, what it does.
  • Its help link or email works, and reaches you.
  • Its screenshots show it as it is, with made-up texts.
  • Its words are plain. A description that says what it does, with no web addresses in it and no promises it can't keep.

What keeps one out

  • Another company's name, logo or marks anywhere in a plugin: its name, short line, description, tabs and kinds, faces, buttons, settings, icon or screenshots; or in your username or help address. A plugin that sorts a bank's texts can say "bank", not the bank's name.
  • Looking like Uplink or a built-in plugin: its name, icon, colours or words.
  • Screenshots showing real people's messages, names or numbers, yours included.
  • Links to anything but your own pages. A plugin's one link is for its help or its own site.
  • Anything hateful, sexual or harassing, in any of its words or pictures.
  • Replies that aren't plain and polite. A button that writes a reply writes words the person sends as their own.

Reports

Anyone who has a plugin, or sees it in the Marketplace, can report it from its page with Report this plugin: why (it's misleading; it's harmful or abusive; it's spam; it asks too much of their texts; it doesn't work; or something else) and a few words. Their username goes with it only if they switch on Send my username with it. Every report is read, by a person, as soon as we can, and the harmful ones first. A report can lead to a plugin being taken off the Marketplace, a version being withdrawn, or its developer being blocked.

Unlisting, withdrawing and blocking

  • Unlisting takes a plugin off the Marketplace: nobody new can add it, and people who have it keep it. You can do it yourself from My submissions; review can do it too, and then only review can list it again.
  • Withdrawing is for a version that should never have been approved. Every device that has it switches it off and says so ("Trips was withdrawn from the Marketplace and is off."), and its page gives the reviewer's reason: "Withdrawn from the Marketplace: … It's off, and does nothing until you remove it." It offers only Remove, and Update where a newer version is approved. The developer sees Withdrawn by review, with the same reason.
  • An approved update doesn't withdraw the version before it unless review chooses to, so nobody's plugin stops working because they haven't updated yet.
  • Blocking a developer stops them submitting ("This account can't submit plugins."), and may take their plugins off the Marketplace too.
