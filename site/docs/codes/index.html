Codes

Sign-in codes in a tab of their own, a tap to copy.

Codes moves sign-in codes (the "your code is 482913" texts) out of your conversations into a Codes tab of their own, newest first. Tap one to copy it.

On from the start. Remove it from its page in the Marketplace.

Copy a code

  • A code's notification has a Copy button too.
  • Long-press a conversation for Move to Codes or Keep in Messages.
  • Telegram's own sign-in codes go here as well.

What counts as a code

Four to eight digits in a text that speaks of a code, a PIN, a password or signing in, from a number that isn't in your contacts. A friend's "the door code is 4417" stays a conversation.

With Ghost Keyboard on your phone, Settings › Ghost Keyboard can offer it your codes, so they're a tap away as you type.
