Beside Google Messages

Keep Google Messages for RCS and use Uplink for everything else.

You don't have to make Uplink your texting app. Beside Google Messages, Google Messages keeps sending and receiving, RCS included, and Uplink shows every conversation alongside your friends, circles and other chats. Replies you write in Uplink go out through Google Messages.

Making Uplink your texting app? Turn off RCS first

If you leave RCS chats on in Google Messages, people who use RCS keep sending you chats there that Uplink can't see. Turn it off in Google Messages: your profile picture, Messages settings › RCS chats.

Set up RCS beside Google Messages

Go to Settings › Messages › Set up RCS. Uplink checks each step and ticks it when it's done.

  1. Google Messages on this phone

    Uplink uses it for RCS. It's free from the Play Store: Get Google Messages if it isn't there.

  2. Google Messages is the default SMS app

    In Android's Settings › Apps › Default apps › SMS app, choose Google Messages.

  3. RCS chats are on in Google Messages

    In Google Messages, tap your profile picture, then Messages settings › RCS chats, and turn it on. It can take a few minutes to say Connected.

  4. Allow Uplink to read and send texts

    Say yes when Uplink asks.

  5. Allow notification access

    That's how RCS chats reach Uplink: it reads Google Messages' notifications. Only Google Messages' are read; every other app's are ignored. Turn it on for Uplink.

    Restricted settings

    If Android won't let you switch it on, tap Open Android's page for Uplink, then ⋮ › Allow restricted settings, and try again.

  6. Leave Google Messages' notifications on, but silent

    Uplink reads them, hides each one as it comes, and shows its own. Turned off completely, Uplink would see nothing.

When every step is ticked: RCS is set up. Uplink shows every conversation, RCS and texts, and replies go through Google Messages.

On your computer

For full history, pictures and reactions on your Mac or Windows PC, open Uplink there, go to Settings › Google Messages, and scan the QR code it shows with Google Messages on your phone (Device pairing in its menu). Your phone needs to stay on and online.

Good to know

  • Google Messages' notifications are the data: if they're off, Uplink sees nothing new.
  • Some features of Google Messages may change without warning, since this relies on what it shows.
