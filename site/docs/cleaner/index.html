Cleaner

Delete conversations in bulk by kind.

Cleaner deletes conversations in bulk, by kind: spam, codes, sales, deliveries and the rest.

On from the start.

  1. Tap the brush

    It's in the list's header.

  2. Tick the kinds, then Delete

It can't be undone

Cleaner deletes whole conversations from the phone, for every app. Archived conversations are left alone.
