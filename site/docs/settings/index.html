Privacy and settings

Settings, at a glance

Every section of Settings, and what it holds.

Everything in Settings, group by group. Search at the top of Settings finds any choice by its name. Sections marked phone are on the Android phone only; a computer has This Mac or This PC in their place.

Plugins

Plugins aren't in Settings: they're in the Marketplace, the store ring on the rail between Archive and Settings, and each plugin's choices are on its page there. Searching Settings for a plugin, or for one of its choices, opens it in the Marketplace. The Marketplace.
