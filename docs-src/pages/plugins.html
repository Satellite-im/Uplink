Plugins are extras you choose: sort your texts, translate them, record calls, watch together. Some are on from the start; others you add when you want them. They're all in the Marketplace, the store on the rail between Archive and Settings. Everything a plugin does happens on your device unless its page says otherwise.

Open the Marketplace

Tap the store on the rail, or in the dock if you use one; on Dial-Up's taskbar it's Market. On a phone it opens on its list: Search the Marketplace, then Discover (every plugin), Added (yours), Updates while one is waiting, and under Make your own, Submit a plugin, My submissions and How to build a plugin. On a computer, or a Fold opened out, Discover opens beside the list. The ring's number is how many updates are waiting.

Add or remove a plugin

In Discover, featured ones are at the top, then the ones you've added, then More to add, then those for circles. The chips pick one shelf: All, Added, For circles, From developers, and each category. The search finds a plugin by its name, its words, its tags or who made it, and so does Search the Marketplace.

Tap a plugin for its page: what it adds, how it looks, What it reads and keeps, and Add to Uplink or Remove. Once it's added, its settings are on its page too. Plugins' settings aren't in Settings any more, but searching Settings for a plugin, or for one of its settings, opens it here.

A plugin from another developer has Help from the developer on its page too, their help link or email, and Report this plugin. Removing one asks first, Remove Trips or Keep it: it's deleted from that device, and your choices for it are kept if you add it again.

A tab a plugin made leads back to it: press and hold the tab, or right-click it on a computer, for About and the plugin's name, which opens its page here.

Plugins from other developers

Besides the built-in plugins, the Marketplace has plugins made by other people. Each one is reviewed by Uplink before it's listed.

  • Their cards say who made them, By @maya, with the Reviewed mark; their pages say Reviewed by Uplink. Built-in plugins say Built in.
  • Adding one asks first, in a sheet that says everything it can do, made from the plugin itself: what a plugin may do.
  • They only ever see texts from numbers not in your contacts, on the one device they're added on: Reads texts from numbers not in your contacts. Never your contacts' texts, group texts, encrypted texts, your own, chats on other networks, or circles and spaces, and nothing at all while Uplink can't read your contacts. And they Can't reach the internet: a button you press can open the plugin's page in your browser, or copy words to your clipboard.
  • They never act on a text with a sign-in code in it.
  • Built-in plugins sort first: a text Codes, Spam filter or a kind of Kinds of text takes stays theirs. To let a plugin's tab have bills or parcels, switch off that kind in Marketplace › Kinds of text.
  • Something wrong with one? Report this plugin on its page. Every report is read.
  • They aren't on iPhone: "Plugins from other developers aren't on iPhone."

Want to make one? Build a plugin. A plugin someone is making shows In development · not reviewed, only on their own device, while Developer mode is on.

Updates

  • When a plugin you've added has a new version, the Marketplace's ring shows a number, and Marketplace › Updates lists it, with what's new and Update. Update all updates those that can't do anything more than before. With none waiting it says Everything's up to date.
  • An update that can do more than the version you have asks first: "Update Trips?", with only what it can now do.
  • A plugin's page says Update to 1.1.0 too, where one is waiting. Updates are never put in without you, and an update keeps a plugin on or off as you had it.
  • A plugin that's withdrawn from the Marketplace switches itself off, and the app says so. Its page gives the reason and offers Remove. One this version of the app can't check or run says why on its page, marked Withdrawn or Not working on its card, with Remove.

For a whole circle

Some built-in plugins (marked Circle below) can be turned on for everyone in a circle, so every member has them in its spaces without adding them. Whoever may change the circle turns them on in Circle settings › Plugins. Plugins from other developers are for one device, and can't be turned on for a circle.
