Uplink runs on a Mac and on Windows as well as your phone. On its own, a computer has your friends, circles, calls, files and your Telegram and Signal chats. Paired with your Android phone, it has your texts too.

Install

  • Mac: download the disk image from gogho.st, open it, drag Uplink to Applications. Signed and checked by Apple.
  • Windows: download and run ghost-messaging-setup.exe. Signed.

Both update themselves. See Install the app.

The first time

  • Sign in to your Uplink account to be the same you as on your phone: same friends, same circles. See Your account.
  • Link an Android phone to see and send your texts: see Pair a computer with your phone. It's optional.

Voice messages

Click the microphone beside the message box, in a text, a Telegram or other network chat, a friend's chat or a space. The box becomes the recorder: a red dot, how long it has run, a meter of how loud you are, and the most it can run. Click Send to send it, or the bin to throw it away. Enter sends and Esc throws it away too.

  • The first time, your computer asks to let Uplink use the microphone. On a Mac, if you said no, allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. On Windows, turn on Settings › Privacy & security › Microphone › Let desktop apps access your microphone.
  • A text's voice message is sent by your phone, so it can be as long as your carrier allows. Anywhere else one can run for up to five minutes.
  • It's the same kind of clip your phone records, so phones play the ones you send from a computer, and the computer plays theirs.
  • On Linux voice messages play, but can't be recorded yet.

The title bar

On a Mac the bar across the top of the window names what's open: the circle you're in, or the conversation you're reading. With neither open it says Uplink. The Window menu and Mission Control call the window the same.

Keyboard

  • Enter sends; Shift+Enter starts a new line.
  • Esc closes a picture or menu, or throws away the voice message you're recording.
  • Drag files onto a Files space, or a conversation, to send them.
