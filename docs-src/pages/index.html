Uplink is one app for the people you talk to: your texts, your friends on Uplink, your circles with their chats, calls and files, and your Telegram, Signal and other chats, side by side. Messages between Uplink apps are sealed end to end, there are no ads, and the app is open source.

It works well enough to use every day, but things still change quickly and some features are marked . If you're testing it, start with Join the alpha, and tell us anything odd in the Alpha testers circle.

Finding your way around

Everything starts from the rail down the left of the screen, the same on every page, as Discord and Slack have. At its head is Messages, your conversations. Under it are your circles, each as its picture: tap one to open it, and a halo in its colour marks the one that's open. A dot beside a circle means something's unread; a red number means someone mentioned you. The circled plus under them starts a new circle or joins one by its link. At the foot: New (start a conversation, a group, a space or a circle), Contacts, Archive, the Marketplace (plugins, as a store) and Settings.

Hold a pointer over any of them for its name. Right-click one, or press and hold it on a phone, for its menu: a circle's has its settings and members, Archive's marks everything in it read, and the Marketplace's and Settings' jump to a section.

The rail is how you move around the app from 0.9.36. Settings › Developer › Navigation can put back a dock along the foot instead: One dock is the same on every page, and Changing dock is the earlier way, where a circle had a dock of its own.

At the top of Messages are three tabs: Messages (texts and chats), Codes (sign-in codes, kept out of the way) and Circles.

A few words we use

WordWhat it means
CircleA group of people with a sidebar of spaces, a bit like a small server. You can be in as many as you like.
SpaceA room in a circle (or on its own): chat and voice, text only, files, or an SMS space for people without the app.
FriendSomeone you talk to over Uplink rather than by text: added by their username, or by their number once you've sealed your texts together.
ConnectionsYour other chat accounts linked into Uplink: Telegram, Signal, WhatsApp, Matrix.
RoutesHow messages travel between Uplink apps: over the relay, or by text.
PluginAn extra you add from the Marketplace: for yourself, or, built in, for everyone in a circle.

On which devices

  • Android phone: everything, including your texts (SMS and picture messages).
  • Mac, Windows and Linux: friends, circles, calls, files and your other chats on their own, and your texts when paired with your Android phone.
  • iPhone : friends, circles and calls. Apple doesn't let apps send or read texts outside the EU, so no texts.
