On a Mac or PC, and on a phone or tablet with a keyboard, the app can be used from the keys. Every shortcut can be moved to other keys in Settings › Keyboard shortcuts. Below, Cmd is Ctrl on Windows, Linux and a phone, and Option is Alt.

The shortcuts

Cmd+KJump to a conversation. Type a name or a number, then Enter. It finds the app's places too: Contacts, Settings and so on.
Option+Down / UpNext / previous conversation in the list, as it's shown.
Cmd+FSearch the conversations.
Cmd+NStart something new: a message, a group, a space or a circle.
Cmd+Shift+RMark all as read in the tab shown.
Cmd+\Show or hide the list beside a conversation, on a wide window.
Cmd+WClose the conversation or page open beside the list.
Cmd+1 … 5Messages, Contacts, Archive, Spaces, Marketplace.
Cmd+,Settings.
Cmd+/This list, in Settings.

Writing

Cmd+BBold
Cmd+IItalic
Cmd+ECode

They put the formatting marks round the words you've chosen, or, with nothing chosen, around what you type next. They work in chats with friends, groups and spaces. A text message shows the marks as written, so they aren't added there.

In a call

Cmd+Shift+MMute or unmute
Cmd+Shift+DDeafen or undeafen
Cmd+Shift+ELeave the call
`Push to talk, while it's switched on: hold it to talk.

Change a shortcut

Tap a shortcut, choose Change the keys, press the keys you want all together, and tap Save. Esc leaves it as it was.

  • If another shortcut is already on those keys, it says so before you save. Saving moves the keys over, and the other shortcut is left on no key until you give it some.
  • Copy, paste, cut, select all, undo, redo and quit can't be taken.
  • Use no key turns a shortcut off. Put back returns one to its own keys, and Put every shortcut back does all of them.
  • A shortcut on a key alone, like push to talk on `, waits while you're typing, so the key still types. One with Cmd, Ctrl or Option works anywhere.

Shortcuts are kept on each device: a change on your Mac doesn't change your PC's.
