Friends are people you talk to over Uplink itself, not by text: messages go over the internet, sealed end to end, with no texting plan involved. There are two ways to become friends.

Add a friend by username

In Settings › Me › Profile, so friends know it's you. See Your profile.

Open Contacts in the rail and tap Add a friend, beside the search. Type their username; it's looked up as you type.

Friends show in Contacts under On Uplink. Tap one to write to them. To remove a friend, open your chat's settings, or their card.

Someone who uses another relay server than you can be a friend too: type their username with their relay after it, @maya@relay.example. See Friends on another relay for what that reaches.

Friends by number

Someone you text who also has Uplink can become a friend by number. First turn on encryption with them; then the conversation offers Add friend. From then on your messages go over the internet instead of as texts, sealed the same way, and reach them even on Wi-Fi without signal.

Groups of friends

New › New group makes a group from your friends. It isn't a group text: messages go over Uplink, the group has a name, members and admins, and it comes with its own space for calls.

Being found

Verify your number and link your other accounts in Settings › Me so friends can find you, and switch on Tell friends I'm here. Settings › Friends joining tells you when people you know join.
