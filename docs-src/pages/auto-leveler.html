Auto leveler brings everyone in a call to about the same loudness on your device. The friend who always sounds far away comes up, and the one shouting into their microphone comes down, so you stop changing volumes in the middle of a call.

Add it: Marketplace › Auto leveler › Add, or turn it on for a whole circle in Circle settings › Plugins.

How it works

Once someone has talked for a few seconds, Auto leveler knows how loud they arrive and turns them up or down to match everyone else. It moves slowly, so a laugh or a cough doesn't swing anyone, and never more than about 9 dB either way, so a whisper stays a whisper.

It changes only what your device plays. Nothing is sent, and nobody else in the call hears anything different.
