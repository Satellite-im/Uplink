Voice changer sends your voice messages in another voice. It keeps how you said it: your timing, your tone, a laugh or a whisper. It uses ElevenLabs, with your own ElevenLabs account.

Add it: Marketplace › Voice changer › Add.

While this is on, each voice message you record goes from your device to ElevenLabs to be changed, then is sent. That recording is not private to you and the people you send it to, and it counts against your ElevenLabs plan. Uplink's servers never see your key or the recording.

Set it up

At elevenlabs.io, open Developers › API keys and create one. If you restrict the key, let it use Voice Changer and read Voices.

Open Settings › Voice changer › ElevenLabs key, paste the key and tap Keep this key. The app checks it with ElevenLabs first. It's kept on this device only and never shown again.

You see every voice in your ElevenLabs account: ElevenLabs' own and any you made or saved there. Tap play to hear a sample, and tap a voice to choose it.

Send a voice message

Record a voice message as usual in a space or a chat on your Android phone. A line under the recording says whose voice it goes in. When you send, it takes a few seconds to change, then goes.

  • It works in spaces and in chats on other networks (Telegram, Signal and the rest). Voice messages in ordinary texts still go in your own voice.
  • If a recording can't be changed (no connection, or your plan has run out), it is not sent, and you're told why. It never goes out in your own voice by mistake.

In calls, live

Turn on Change my voice in calls, live and, in a space's call, everyone hears you in the voice you chose. ElevenLabs needs a whole phrase before it can change it, so each thing you say is heard a second or two after you say it, at your pauses (or every four seconds if you keep talking).

  • Your own voice is never heard in the call while it's on. Between phrases, others hear silence.
  • Nothing is sent while you're muted, and what you say while muted is dropped.
  • If ElevenLabs can't change a phrase (no connection, or your plan has run out), the line over the call turns red and says why. You stay silent rather than heard as yourself; tap My own voice to go back.
  • It costs what your ElevenLabs plan charges for every minute you talk.

Settings

Forget the key takes the key, the voice and the list of voices off this device. To stop the key working anywhere, delete it in your ElevenLabs account too.
