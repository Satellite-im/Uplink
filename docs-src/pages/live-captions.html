Live captions shows what everyone in a call says, written out as they talk, each line named. Each person's words are written out on their own device and shared with the call, so nobody's voice goes to a server.

Add it: Marketplace › Live captions › Add, or for a whole circle.

Turn captions on

In a call, open the call's menu and tap Show captions. Hide captions turns them off again. It's for you alone; others choose for themselves.
