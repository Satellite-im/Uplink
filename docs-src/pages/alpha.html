This page carries your invite to the Alpha testers circle. Step 5 opens it.

First, a few questions

What will you test on?

The iPhone app isn't out yet. Pick a computer for now and you'll still get the whole circle: chat, voice, video and files.

Will Uplink be your texting app?

It's an alpha: running it beside your current app is the safe way to start. You can make it your texting app any time in Settings.

Then, five steps

  1. Download the app

    The Android app comes straight from us, not the Play Store.

    A disk image for Macs on Apple silicon or Intel, macOS 12 or later.

    An installer for Windows 10 and 11.

    Pick your device above for the right download.

    Download for Android Download for Mac Download for Windows

  2. Install it

    • Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Files › Downloads.
    • If Android says your browser can't install apps, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
    • If Play Protect asks about an unknown app, tap More details › Install anyway.
    • Open the downloaded disk image, then drag Uplink into Applications.
    • Open it from Applications. It updates itself from then on.
    • Run ghost-messaging-setup.exe and follow the installer.
    • Open Uplink from the Start menu. It updates itself from then on.

  3. Pick a username and password

    The first screen asks for two things: a username, which is how the other testers find you, and a password, which makes your Uplink account so you can sign in on another device. Tap Continue.

    Pick a username and a password. Continue Already have an account? Sign in

    First, one page about your texts (below). Then a username, which is how the other testers find you, and a password, which makes your Uplink account so you can sign in on another device. Tap Continue.

    On the texts page, tap Use beside Google Messages. Your texts stay where they are, and you don't need to make Uplink your texting app.

    Set as default SMS app Use beside Google Messages

    On the texts page, tap Set as default SMS app and say yes when Android asks.

    If Android refuses, tap Open Android's page for Uplink, then ⋮ › Allow restricted settings, and try again.

    While they're on in Google Messages, people who use RCS keep sending you chats that never arrive as texts. The welcome shows a Coming from Google Messages? Do this first card that opens the right setting.

    Next come your 12 recovery words. Write them down if you can: with them you can get your account back without your password. Then tap them in order to check, or tap Skip this check and you're in. You can see them again any time in Settings › Me › Account.

    Already have an Uplink account? Tap Sign in under Already have an account? on that same screen instead. You don't need to pair a phone to join the circle; skip it if Uplink offers it. More in The first time you open it.

    If you joined before, you went through six pages and made your account later in Settings. New testers now pick a username and password on the first screen and are done. Nothing changes for you.

  4. Add your name and picture

    Go to Settings › Me › Profile and add a name and picture, so the other testers know who's talking.

    Verifying your number and linking other accounts can wait. See Your profile and number.

  5. Join the Alpha testers circle

    Tap Open the invite on this phone. Uplink opens on the circle's invite: tap Join.

    Tap Copy the invite, then in Uplink tap New › Join a circle by link, paste it, and tap Join.

    Open the invite

    • Tap it on your phone and Uplink opens on the invite: tap Join.
    • Or copy it, then in Uplink tap New › Join a circle by link, paste it, and tap Join.

Updates arrive in the app by themselves. Each one says what's new, so look out for things to try.

Telling us what you find

  • Post it in the Alpha testers circle: what you did, what you expected, and what happened, with a screenshot if you can.
  • For a problem with texts or pictures, include the report from Settings › Developer › Copy report: it says what the app tried, never what your messages said.
