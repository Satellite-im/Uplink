An SMS space is a circle's chat that people without the app can be in, by text. One member's Android phone does the texting: it texts each message to everyone in it by text, and brings their replies back into the space.

How it works

  • Every message written in the space is texted from the texter's phone to each person in it by text, as Name: words. A picture or file is said in words, like (a file: menu.pdf).
  • What they text back comes into the space marked 📱, so everyone in the app sees it.
  • Each person is texted on their own, so nobody's number goes to the others.
  • The texts are sent from the texter's number and count on their phone plan.
📱 Mum: Dinner at 6? Yes, I'll bring dessert Alex: Yes, I'll bring dessert Dinner at 6?

Make one

In the circle, tap New space and choose SMS space. Your phone becomes its texter.

It must be an Android phone with Uplink as its texting app, and Uplink must be running for texts to go and come. Only that phone knows the numbers of the people in it.

Open the space, tap People by text in its header, and under Add someone by text enter a name and number, then Add and text them. They get a text saying how it works and how to leave.

Add and text them

Members with a verified number are in it by themselves

Members of the circle who have verified their number don't need adding: when their app comes into the space, it gives the texter's phone their number, sealed so only that phone can read it, and they're texted from then on. They aren't texted their own messages. People by text marks them a member, by their linked number.

Leaving

  • Anyone in it by text can text STOP to leave. They're told, and stay out.
  • The texter can take someone out: in People by text, tap them, then Take out of the space. They stay out too, until added again by hand.
