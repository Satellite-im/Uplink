A circle is a group of people with a sidebar of its own spaces: chat and voice, text, files, or an SMS space for people without the app. Think of a friend group, a club, a team or a family. Everything said in it is sealed end to end, so only its members can read it.

Make a circle

Tap the circled plus under your circles in the rail, then New circle. New at the rail's foot has New circle too.

New circleJoin a circle by link

Give it a name, a colour and an icon. A picture of your own is optional, shown on the circle's face.

Under First spaces the circle starts with a couple of spaces. Press one to rename it or change what it's for; add more, Files spaces too, any time.

Under Bring friends, tick friends to invite straight away. You can invite anyone else afterwards.

Invite people

Open the circle, tap its name at the top, then Members, then Add people. (Or Invite people in the same menu.)

Join a circle

  • From a friend's invite: a card shows in Messages. Tap Join.
  • From a link: tap it on your phone and Uplink opens on the invite. Or copy it, then New › Join a circle by link, paste, and Join.
  • Without the app yet: the link opens a page at gogho.st/join to get it. Install it, pick a username, and the app opens the circle.

Inside a circle

A circle opens in the list's place: its banner and name at the top and its spaces under their headings. The rail stays where it is, with a bar beside the circle that's open.

  • Tap the circle's name for its menu: Members, Invite people, New space and Arrange spaces (if your role may), Circle settings and Leave circle. Right-clicking its picture in the rail, or pressing and holding it, opens the same menu.
  • A space with people in its call lists them under it, each with their picture and name. While you're in that call too, whoever is talking has a green ring.
  • Messages at the top of the rail takes you back to your conversations; another circle's picture opens that circle.
  • Scroll the spaces up and the banner shrinks to just the name, then grows back at the top.

Plugins for the whole circle

In Circle settings › Plugins, whoever may change the circle can turn on plugins for everyone in it: Record call, Live captions, Watch together, Soundboard, Spotify, Notion and others. For a whole circle has the list.

Leave or delete a circle

In Circle settings, Leave circle takes you out; you can come back with an invite. The owner can Delete circle, which takes it away for everyone.
