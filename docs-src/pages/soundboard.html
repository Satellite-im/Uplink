A circle's own sounds

Whoever may change a circle can add up to 16 sounds of its own. Open Circle settings › Plugins › Soundboard › Its sounds, tap Add a sound, choose an audio file, and give it a name and a face.

  • A clip is an MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, Opus or FLAC file of at most 1 MB. A few seconds is plenty: a sound stops after ten.
  • Press and hold one of the circle's pads to name it again or take it away.
  • Everyone in the circle gets its sounds; they show under the app's own in a call there.

What you hear

Under the pads, On this device has the soundboard's loudness, and Hear what others play. With that off you hear none of it, and still see who played what. Deafened in a call, you hear no sounds either.

Pressing a pad tells the call which sound it was. Each device plays its own copy, so only the app's sounds and the ones the circle added can be played. Nobody can play more than a few in a row.
