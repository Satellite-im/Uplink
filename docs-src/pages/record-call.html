Record call records a space's call to one video file (MP4): first everyone together, then each voice on a track of its own, named for them. Edit one person's voice without the others, or listen to just one. Everyone in the call is asked first, and only people who say yes are in it.

Add it: Marketplace › Record call › Add, or for a whole circle in Circle settings › Plugins. Works on Android, Mac, iPhone and Windows.

Record a call

While you're in a space's call, a Record button sits in the space's header, beside its settings. It's also in the call's menu.

A red line goes over the call for everyone. Each person's app asks whether their voice may be in it. Until they tap Include me, it isn't.

A line in the space's chat says it too, so people on older versions know.

Tap Mixer on your red line. It fills the screen with each voice as a track: its waveform growing as the call goes on, a meter, and mute and solo. Switch to the mixer face for a fader per voice, to set how loud each is in the track of everyone mixed. Tap the arrow at the top to close it; it closes by itself when the recording ends.

Tap Stop (in red, in the same place). The file is made on your device: it takes a moment. You're told where it went.

Where recordings go

  • On your device, always: in Recordings (from the call's menu), to play, share, save a copy, or delete.
  • In a circle, also into a folder of one of its Files spaces, for everyone in the circle. Until the circle chooses, that's its first Files space, in a folder named Recordings. If the circle has no Files space, or you can't add files there, it stays on your device and says why.

Mix a recording again

Tap a recording, then Open in the mixer. It opens like the mixer during a call: each voice as a track with its waveform, a meter, mute and solo, and a fader per voice on the mixer face. Tap Play to hear it as the faders are set. Tap anywhere on the tracks to jump there.

Tap Save mix to keep it: a new recording is made beside the first, with everyone mixed as you set it and each voice still on its own track. The first recording isn't changed. The new one says mixed again in the list.

The first time, each voice takes a moment to open. After that it opens straight away. On iPhone, a fader can turn a voice down but not up past how it was recorded.

Consent and the law

Everyone is told and asked, and nobody's voice is in a recording without their yes. Laws on recording conversations differ by place; asking is on you as well as the app.
